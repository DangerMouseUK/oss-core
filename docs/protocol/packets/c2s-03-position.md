## Layout

| Offset | Size | Type | Field | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 1 | u8 | type | `0x03` |

| 10 | 1 | u8 | checksum | Makes the XOR of bytes 0–21 zero |

| 20 | 2 | u16 | weapon | Weapon word |
| 22 | 10 | extra | extra | Extra position data, 32-byte form only (not covered by the checksum) |

Integers are little-endian. `n` is a variable length; `i` counts repeated entries from 0.

## Notes

x = y = −1 means dead, waiting to respawn. Clients add the extra data while S2C `0x1C` (2-byte form) is on.
